Snug Bento level editor

Level JSON (LevelDef). w, h; mask: w·h chars row by row, . box cell, space outside, g garnish. walls: cell·2 (right edge) or cell·2+1 (bottom edge). pieces: {c: [[x,y]…], s?: soft shape, t: 0/1 hot/2 cold, sauce?: piece}. sol: one placement per piece {s, r, x, y}. Pieces show turned as stored; the preview places the solution with "Show solution".

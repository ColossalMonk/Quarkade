Aisle Nine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Aisle Nine is a puzzle game made by [developer name] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, power-ups, Main Street displays and shops, stands, settings and Daily Restock results) is saved on your device. On some web game sites, the site may also keep a copy of this save so you can continue on another device. We do not ask for your name, email or any account.

Advertising

The game shows ads. On web game sites, ads are provided by the site that hosts the game (for example CrazyGames, Playgama, GameDistribution, GamePix, GameMonetize or YouTube), under that site's own privacy policy. In the mobile app, ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) the app asks for your consent first, and on iPhone it asks for permission before tracking. You can change your choice in your device's privacy settings. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases in the mobile app are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your progress from the game's settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app at any time.
